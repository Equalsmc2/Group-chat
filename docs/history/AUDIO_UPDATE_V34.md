# Soul Seekers v34 — Audio System

## How it works

Soul Seekers now has two audio layers:

1. **UI SFX** — local, low-volume feedback for tabs, buttons, confirms, selects, and director controls.
2. **Cinematic SFX** — player-facing effects for Combat, scene transitions, Stage/presentation events, and DM-fired cues.

Cinematic SFX are intended to play from the **armed TV**. In DM Control, enable **Preview cinematic SFX on this laptop** only if you want the laptop to mirror them.

## Automatic cues

- New turn: turn cut-in sting.
- Light damage: short impact.
- Heavy damage: rotating impact variants.
- Critical threshold / 0 HP / death-save failure: critical hit sound.
- Healing: manifestation/heal sound.
- New condition: restrained impact accent.
- Spell-slot spend / concentration start: casting accent.
- TV scene change: scene transition sting.
- Blackout/restore: dark wash.
- Boss/critical/victory/revelation overlays: matching cinematic cue.

## DM Sound Deck

Audio Director contains primary buttons for Turn Sting, Impact, Spell Hit, Gunshot, Lightning, Critical, Boss Reveal, and Victory. **More Cues** exposes Bow, Gun Cock, Reload, Revolver Spin, Spell Charge, Elemental, Summon, and Void Wash.

## Music ducking

Important cinematic sounds temporarily lower the current YouTube music volume, then restore it. The session music volume remains authoritative.

## Browser behavior

The TV still requires one click on **ARM DISPLAY + AUDIO** because browsers require a user gesture before autoplaying session audio. That same click unlocks the SFX engine.
