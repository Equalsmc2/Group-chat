# Dead Air visual smoke check

Launch `TEST_ASYA_BREAKCORE.html` from a local web server, then press **FULL DEAD AIR** and **SHORT / 500MS**.

Full should present neon-blue/pink frame collapse with a dramatic Asya portrait reveal, then resolve into the sustained altered world.

Short should be an anonymous half-second neon-blue/pink corruption over the ordinary interface. There is deliberately **no portrait, label, name, quote, or description**. It must fully disappear without dimming or leaving a permanent overlay.

The preview uses placeholder campaign UI and intentionally does not connect to Firestore or play any sounds.
