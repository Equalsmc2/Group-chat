# Scene Lab — quick start

1. Open **DM Control** → **Scene Lab** (first workspace).
2. Under **Editing background for**, choose **The Stage**, **Terminal**, **Combat**, or **DM Control**.
3. Click one of the 20 Boston photo thumbnails. The larger thumbnail shows your selection, but it is not sent yet.
4. Press **Apply to Selected Scene** to publish just that scene's background. To set a global photo, press **Apply to All Scenes**.
5. **Surprise Me** chooses a candidate for review; it will not broadcast until you press Apply. **Restore Default** affects only the selected scene.
6. To add a photo from your PC, use **Use Your Own Background**. The image is compressed to WebP and stored in the shared scene state. It is intentionally size-limited and the original is not uploaded.
7. Use **World Lighting** and **World Weather** for a shared day/night and weather change. In PHCode local preview, the desktop and its TV tab sync in the same browser origin. In deployed cloud mode, connected signed-in devices synchronize through Firestore.
8. Use **Quick Stage Captions** to load a template, edit the Stage headline/caption, then press **Update Stage** (no scene switch) or **Update + Take Live** (broadcasts Stage).

The **Stage illustration URL** in Cue Studio remains separate: a foreground illustration can still cover the chosen scenic background. For best results, provide photos no larger than 12 MB, and use wide (landscape) images.
