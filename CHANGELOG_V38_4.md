## Behavior
- Full Asya LA now:
  - plays a short radio chirp,
  - enters a technical broadcast-collapse visual state,
  - loops the **Blood Drain** track until the LA is ended,
  - keeps the screen color-inverted until the LA is ended.
- Instant / Short Asya trigger now:
  - applies a brief static burst,
  - flashes the inverted-color effect,
  - auto-clears after a short duration.
